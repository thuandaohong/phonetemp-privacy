Privacy Policy — Phone Temp

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Phone Temp ("the app", package com.dth.studio.phonetemp) shows your phone's CPU, GPU, battery and surface temperatures, keeps a temperature history on your phone, and can alert you when a part gets too hot. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does (and does not) do with that information.

Summary

Phone Temp does not collect or upload your temperature readings, history or device information. Everything the app measures is shown and stored only on your phone. There are no accounts, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

Two Google services used by the app send limited data off-device, and neither of them receives your temperature readings:

  • Ads (Google AdMob) in the free version — see Advertising below.
  • Google Play for the optional Pro purchase and for the in-app rating prompt — see Purchases and ratings below.

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

What the app reads and stores (and why)

All of the following is read on-device and shown only in the app, its notification, its widgets or its floating overlay. None of it is uploaded or shared.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS or storage permissions.

Files you export or share

When you export a report (PDF or XLSX) it is saved to your phone's Downloads folder; a shared gaming summary image is saved to Pictures/PhoneTemp. They leave your phone only if you choose to share them through Android's share sheet, to the app you pick.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob (banner and native ads; optionally a rewarded ad you choose to watch to unlock a feature). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google your device advertising ID (AD_ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), ad interactions (views and taps) and limited technical data (such as device model and Android version). The app itself holds no location permission. This is governed by Google's privacy policy https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising notice https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the EEA, UK and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Settings › Privacy › Ads. Buying Pro removes all ads; ads are also never shown while a gaming session is recording.

Purchases and ratings

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app only learns whether you own Pro and never sees your payment details. The rating prompt uses Google Play In-App Review; your rating goes directly to Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

All app data lives on your phone. Clear it at any time in Settings › Apps › Phone Temp › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. History older than 90 days is deleted automatically. Reports or images you exported stay in Downloads / Pictures until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
